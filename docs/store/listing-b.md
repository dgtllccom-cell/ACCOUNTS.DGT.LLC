## English
**Title (30):** DGT.llc B
**Short description (80):** Your DGT business ERP on your phone — accounts, purchase, sales, stock, reports.
**Full description:**
DGT.llc B is the mobile front door to the DGT business ERP you already use on the computer. Sign in with your existing ERP account and open the business modules you are authorised for: accounts and ledgers, cash and roznamcha, purchase, sales, inventory and warehouses, customers and CRM, HR and general office, reports with print and PDF.
• One ERP — the same data, login, permissions and country/branch limits as the web version; nothing is copied to the phone.
• Five languages with full right-to-left layout: English, اردو, العربية, فارسی, پښتو.
• Use the camera to attach documents, receive notifications, and work on phones and tablets.
• Secure HTTPS connection to the company ERP only. Accounts are issued by your administrator.
