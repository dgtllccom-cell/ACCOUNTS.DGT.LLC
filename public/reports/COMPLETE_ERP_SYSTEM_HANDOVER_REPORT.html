6. Journal Roznamcha & Cash Entry Engine

The Roznamcha Cash Entry module enables real-time daily ledger recording with automated debit/credit balancing. Every transaction generates four sequential serial numbers: Super Admin Serial, Country Serial, Branch Serial, and Entry Serial for full audit traceability.

7. Bank Roznamcha & Cheque Management System

Complete Bank & Cheque Lifecycle Engine

The Bank Roznamcha module handles all commercial bank transactions, cheque payments, deposits, and bank reconciliations:

  • Cheque Status Lifecycle: Tracks status through PENDING, CLEARED, DISHONORED, and CANCELLED.
  • Clearance Date Tracking: Stores actual date of bank realization separate from voucher entry date.
  • Multi-Currency Banking: Direct support for USD, AED, PKR, AFN, SAR, and EUR bank accounts.
  • Automated Ledger Posting: Automatically syncs with bank ledger upon clearance.
  • Dedicated Filter & Report Engine: Filter by Bank Account, Cheque Number, Clearance Status, Date Range, and Branch.

8. Money Exchange & Currency Conversion Module

The Money Exchange module delivers a full-width financial conversion interface featuring real-time conversion calculators:

  • Multiply/Divide Operational Formulas: Seamlessly toggle between direct and indirect exchange quotations.
  • Historical & Daily USD Rates: Daily rates from daily_usd_rates table auto-populate conversion rates while allowing manual overrides.
  • Dual Posting: Instant generation of debit and credit voucher entries in source and target currency ledgers.

9. Expenses Bills & One-Click Roznamcha Transfer

The Expenses Bill system provides comprehensive utility, rent, freight, custom duty, and operational overhead management:

  • Categorized Bill Entry: Group expenses by category, supplier, vehicle/shipment number, and branch.
  • One-Click Roznamcha Posting: Automatically transfers approved bills into the Daily Roznamcha book as a cash or bank disbursement.
  • Tax & Duty Invoicing: Handles VAT, customs clearance, and withholding taxes natively.

10. Country Purchase: Source Loading, Transportation & Destination Stock

End-to-End Multi-Stage Purchase Supply Chain

The Country Purchase module handles cross-border supply chain operations:

  • Phase 1: Source Purchase Loading: Country source staff record purchase booking, supplier contract, freight details, container/truck numbers, and dispatch weights.
  • Phase 2: In-Transit Transportation Tracking: Tracks shipping agent, transit route, customs clearance at border ports (e.g. Chaman, Torkham, Bandar Abbas).
  • Phase 3: Destination Receiving & Stock Valuation: Destination branch staff verify received quantities, inspect quality, record variances, and automatically update warehouse stock on hand and moving average valuation.
  • Dual-Scope Permissions: RLS-hardened permissions ensure source branches cannot overwrite destination receiving, and vice versa.

11. Sales Orders & Customer Invoicing Lifecycle

The Sales Order module handles wholesale and retail client bookings, multi-currency invoices, advance payment tracking, and balance settlements with automatic dispatch release locking.
