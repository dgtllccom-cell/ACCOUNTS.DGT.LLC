8. Accounting, Roznamcha, Ledger & Reporting Architecture

Roznamcha & Daily Books

The Roznamcha engine records all cash, bank, purchase, and sales transactions in real time with automated dual-entry debit and credit allocation. Each transaction is indexed with Super Admin, Country, Branch, and Entry serial numbers for audit compliance.

General Ledger & Branch Ledgers

Ledger queries aggregate from journal_lines and roznamcha_entries, computing opening balance, running debit, running credit, and closing balance in the account's native currency and consolidated base currency (USD).

Universal Report Modal (Journal/Ledger Print & PDF Engine)

UniversalReportModal Standardized Features

  • Standardized Across 14+ Modules: Companies, Banks, Warehouses, Customers, Accounts, Goods, Employees, Roznamcha, General Ledger, Stock Movements, Ports, Document Types, Registration Types, and Locations.
  • Dual Presentation: Switch between Classic Table View and Formal Journal/Ledger View.
  • RTL-Aware PDF & Print: Automatically rotates headers, debit/credit columns, and signature blocks when Urdu, Arabic, Persian, or Pashto is selected.
  • Summary Metrics: Automatic summation of Total Records, Total Debits, Total Credits, and Net Balance.
