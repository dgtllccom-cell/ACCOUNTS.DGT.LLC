## English
**Title (30):** DGT.llc BS
**Short description (80):** DGT shipping line & clearing agent ERP — BL, containers, tracking, customs bills.
**Full description:**
DGT.llc BS is the mobile front door to the Shipping Line and Clearing Agent side of the DGT ERP. Sign in with your existing ERP account and open the shipping modules you are authorised for: BL entry and reports, shipment and container tracking (Sea, Road, Air and Train, multi-leg), customer orders, customs documents and expenses, clearing agent bills and workspace, handovers and shipping-line statements.
• One ERP — the same data, login, permissions and country/branch limits as the web version; nothing is copied to the phone.
• Search by BL, container, vessel, voyage, customer, shipping line, truck or flight.
• Five languages with full right-to-left layout: English, اردو, العربية, فارسی, پښتو.
• Camera for documents, notifications, phones and tablets. Secure HTTPS connection to the company ERP only; accounts are issued by your administrator.
